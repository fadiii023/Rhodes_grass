Buyer FAQ

What bale sizes are available?

We offer small bales (10-15 kg) and large bales (200-300 kg). Custom sizes may be available upon request for bulk orders.

What is the moisture content of your Rhodes Grass hay?

Our Rhodes Grass is dried to 8-12% moisture content, which is the export standard. This ensures safe storage and prevents spoilage during shipment.

What is Rhodes Grass suitable for?

Rhodes Grass hay is suitable for cattle, sheep, goats, horses, and mixed livestock operations. It is particularly popular for dairy farms due to its nutritional quality and consistent forage availability.

Can you export to Saudi Arabia and GCC countries?

Yes, we regularly export Rhodes Grass hay to Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain, and other international destinations. We handle all export documentation and logistics coordination. Learn more about our export markets →

What documentation is provided with orders?

We provide product specifications, quality certificates, export documentation, and support materials tailored to your destination. Additional documents are available upon request.

How do I request a bulk quote?

Visit our Contact page and complete the quote form with your quantity, destination, and bale preference. You can also reach us via WhatsApp for immediate assistance.

What is your minimum order quantity?

We support bulk orders starting from 50 tons. For larger operations requiring 500+ tons, please contact us directly for specific arrangements.

Are your bales packed for export?

Yes, all bales are packaged and prepared to withstand export shipment. Bales are protected and formatted according to buyer preferences and destination requirements.
